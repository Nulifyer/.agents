## Worker review checkpoint

- Worker and repository:
- Goal and authority: links to owning instructions
- Last reviewed: timestamp, branch/revision, relevant dirty state, session cursor
- Open findings: ID, status, evidence link, worker response or objection
- Agreed next action: owner, expected result, verification, escalation condition
- Next review trigger:

Replace stale entries. Keep only unresolved findings and latest review position.
Link project state and evidence at their existing owners. Finding status is
proposed, sent, agreed, implemented, or verified; mark disputes or withdrawals
explicitly. Record failed or unavailable delivery as unsent. Omit unavailable
host fields rather than inventing values. Do not copy a transcript or test diary.
